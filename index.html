A FEW USEFUL DETAILS

Before you
get started.

A small workspace with a straightforward purpose: make repetitive verification tasks easier to work through.

What is Verification Tools?

Verification Tools is a browser app that brings reusable forms, input formatting, and conditional text templates into one workspace. Use it to prepare reference verification notes and other recurring responses from the details you enter.

Do I need an account or an installation?

No account is required to use the published workspace. Open the tools in your browser, select a category, and enter your details.

Can I create or customize a tool?

Yes. Open Manage tools to edit layouts, create tools, and try the visual editor. Saving applies changes only to your current demo; refreshing restores the published workspace. To keep and publish changes, use the local version and redeploy the app.

Are my entries and generated notes saved?

Inputs and generated text are temporary and are not saved to the tool catalog. Copy anything you need before resetting, switching categories, or leaving or refreshing the workspace.

Does the app verify information automatically?

The app formats the information you provide and applies configured text rules. It does not independently verify a reference or decide an outcome. Review the information and generated notes before using them.
